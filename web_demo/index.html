01 Limitations of Simple Outcome-Based Rewards

How is a homemade pizza made from scratch?

Expected answer: Mix flour, water, yeast, salt, and olive oil into a dough. Knead and let rise, then shape, add tomato sauce, mozzarella, and toppings. Bake in a preheated oven at 240°C for 10–15 minutes until golden.

02 Generalization to Mathematical and Complex Tasks

A jacket originally costs €80. Its price increases by 25%, then decreases by 20%. You buy three jackets and receive a 15% discount on their combined price. An 8% sales tax applies to the discounted jacket total, and a flat €6 shipping fee is added after tax. What is the final amount you pay?

Expected answer: €226.32.

03 Unnecessary Search Calls

Expected answer: Paris.

04 Limitations of the 2018 Wikipedia Dump

05 Robustness to Irrelevant or Misleading Information
